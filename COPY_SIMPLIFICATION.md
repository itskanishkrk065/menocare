# MenoCare Copy Simplification & Language Architecture

## 1. Principles of Patient-Friendly Healthcare Language

1. **Reassure, Never Alarm:** Menopause is a normal, natural life transition. Avoid phrasing that makes ordinary symptoms sound like acute medical emergencies.
2. **Plain English First:** Use the words real women use when speaking with trusted friends or doctors.
3. **Preserve Medical Accuracy:** Do not change clinical meaning, score calculations, or symptom dimensions (e.g. severity vs. frequency).
4. **Sentence Case & Clarity:** Avoid robotic uppercase kickers, buzzwords, and abbreviations.

## 3. Assessment & Questionnaire Overhaul

### MRS (Menopause Rating Scale) 11 Validated Items:
1. **Hot flashes and sweating:** "Sudden waves of heat, sweating episodes, or night chills."
2. **Heart discomfort:** "Heart fluttering, racing, or pounding unexpectedly."
3. **Sleeping problems:** "Trouble falling asleep, waking during the night, or waking up exhausted."
4. **Depressive mood:** "Feeling down, sad, on the verge of tears, or lacking drive."
5. **Irritability:** "Feeling unusually impatient, tense, or easily agitated."
6. **Anxiety:** "Inner restlessness, feeling on edge, or sudden feelings of panic."
7. **Physical and mental exhaustion:** "Feeling drained, sluggish, with lower energy or brain fog."
8. **Sexual problems:** "Changes in sexual desire, satisfaction, or comfort during intimacy."
9. **Bladder problems:** "Needing to pee more frequently, urgently, or difficulty with bladder control."
10. **Vaginal dryness:** "Dryness, burning, irritation, or discomfort."
11. **Joint and muscular discomfort:** "Aching joints, stiffness, or muscle soreness."
